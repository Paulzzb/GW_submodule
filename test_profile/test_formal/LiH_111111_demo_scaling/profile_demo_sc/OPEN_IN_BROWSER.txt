Open this folder in your local browser:

  index.html          (profile summary)
  file0.html          (same as index.html)
  matlab-report-styles.css  (bundled stylesheet)

Remote server: download/copy this entire directory to your PC,
then double-click index.html, or run:
  python3 -m http.server 8765
and visit http://localhost:8765/index.html
